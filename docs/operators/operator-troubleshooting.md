# 🔧 Operator Troubleshooting Guide

Quick solutions for common issues warehouse operators encounter during daily operations. If these solutions don't work, contact your supervisor or IT support.

## 🚨 Emergency Procedures

### System Emergency
**If the system is completely down:**
1. **Don't panic** - Switch to paper backup procedures
2. **Notify supervisor immediately**
3. **Document what you were doing when the issue occurred**
4. **Continue operations using manual processes**

### Safety Emergency
**If equipment malfunctions or safety concerns:**
1. **Stop operations immediately**
2. **Press emergency stop button if available**
3. **Alert supervisor and safety team**
4. **Clear area if necessary**

## 📱 Mobile App Issues

### App Won't Start or Crashes

**Symptoms:** App closes immediately or won't open
**Solutions:**
1. **Force close and restart** the app
2. **Check WiFi connection** - look for WiFi symbol
3. **Restart your mobile device**
4. **Check battery level** - charge if below 20%
5. **Contact IT** if problem persists

**How to force close app:**
- **Android**: Tap recent apps button, swipe up on OpenWES app
- **iOS**: Double-tap home button, swipe up on OpenWES app

### App Running Very Slowly

**Symptoms:** Long delays, screens take forever to load
**Solutions:**
1. **Check WiFi signal strength** - move closer to router if weak
2. **Close other apps** running in background
3. **Restart the app**
4. **Clear app cache** (Android: Settings > Apps > OpenWES > Storage > Clear Cache)
5. **Ask IT to check server status**

### Can't Login to App

**Symptoms:** Login screen shows error or won't accept credentials
**Solutions:**
1. **Double-check username and password** - watch for caps lock
2. **Verify warehouse code** is correct
3. **Check WiFi connection**
4. **Try guest WiFi if available**
5. **Contact supervisor** for password reset

## 📷 Scanning Issues

### Scanner Won't Read Barcodes

**Symptoms:** Beeps but doesn't scan, or no beep at all
**Solutions:**
1. **Clean the camera lens** with soft cloth
2. **Check barcode quality** - is it damaged or dirty?
3. **Improve lighting** - use flashlight or move to brighter area
4. **Hold steady** - keep device still for 2-3 seconds
5. **Try different angle** - tilt device slightly
6. **Restart scanner app**

**Pro Tips:**
- Keep barcodes flat and clean
- Avoid shadows over the barcode
- Don't scan through plastic or glass if possible

### Wrong Item Scanned

**Symptoms:** Scanned different item than intended
**Solutions:**
1. **Use "Cancel" or "Back" button** immediately
2. **Re-scan the correct item**
3. **If stuck, ask supervisor** to help reverse the transaction
4. **Report pattern** if this happens frequently with specific items

### Scanner Light Not Working

**Symptoms:** No flashlight when scanning in dark areas
**Solutions:**
1. **Check flashlight setting** in scanner app
2. **Restart the app**
3. **Check device permissions** for camera/flashlight
4. **Move to better lit area**
5. **Request replacement device** if light is broken

## 🖨️ Printer Problems

### Printer Won't Print Labels

**Symptoms:** Print job sent but nothing prints
**Solutions:**
1. **Check printer power** - look for power light
2. **Check paper supply** - reload if empty
3. **Check ribbon/toner** - replace if needed
4. **Look for paper jams** - gently remove stuck paper
5. **Press printer's "Feed" button** to test
6. **Restart printer** - turn off/on
7. **Contact IT** if still not working

### Poor Print Quality

**Symptoms:** Faded, smeared, or incomplete printing
**Solutions:**
1. **Check ribbon/ink levels** - replace if low
2. **Clean print head** with cleaning card
3. **Adjust print darkness** setting
4. **Check paper type** - use correct label stock
5. **Replace ribbon** even if it looks okay

### Wrong Labels Printing

**Symptoms:** Correct printer but wrong label format
**Solutions:**
1. **Verify you selected correct printer** in the app
2. **Check task details** - make sure you're printing for right order
3. **Contact supervisor** - may be system configuration issue
4. **Don't use incorrect labels** - waste can be expensive

## 💻 Workstation Issues

### Computer/Terminal Frozen

**Symptoms:** Screen won't respond to clicks or keyboard
**Solutions:**
1. **Wait 30 seconds** - system may be processing
2. **Try Ctrl+Alt+Del** (Windows) or Cmd+Option+Esc (Mac)
3. **Restart computer** if completely frozen
4. **Check all cables** are connected properly
5. **Contact IT** if problem repeats

### Keyboard/Mouse Not Working

**Symptoms:** Can't type or move cursor
**Solutions:**
1. **Check USB connections** - unplug and reconnect
2. **Try different USB port**
3. **Check batteries** in wireless devices
4. **Try keyboard/mouse from another workstation**
5. **Restart computer**

### Monitor Issues

**Symptoms:** Black screen, flickering, or wrong colors
**Solutions:**
1. **Check monitor power button**
2. **Check cable connections** to computer
3. **Adjust brightness/contrast** controls
4. **Try different monitor** if available
5. **Call IT support**

## 📦 Operational Issues

### Can't Find Inventory Item

**Symptoms:** System says item is in location but it's not there
**Solutions:**
1. **Double-check location code** - look for similar codes nearby
2. **Check nearby locations** - item may be misplaced  
3. **Look on different shelves** in same location
4. **Check if item was recently moved**
5. **Use "Item Not Found" function** in system
6. **Report to supervisor** for cycle count

### System Shows Wrong Quantity

**Symptoms:** Physical count doesn't match system quantity
**Solutions:**
1. **Recount carefully** - double-check your count
2. **Check if items are in multiple containers**
3. **Look for partial cases or open boxes**
4. **Use quantity adjustment** function if authorized
5. **Report discrepancy** to supervisor immediately

### Pick List Missing Items

**Symptoms:** Can't find items listed on pick list
**Solutions:**
1. **Check alternate locations** listed in system
2. **Verify you're in correct zone**
3. **Look for replenishment in progress**
4. **Mark as "short pick"** and continue
5. **Notify supervisor** about shortage

### Wrong Container/Tote

**Symptoms:** Scanned wrong container for task
**Solutions:**
1. **Don't put items in wrong container**
2. **Use "Cancel Container" function**
3. **Scan correct container**
4. **Ask supervisor** if items already placed incorrectly
5. **Document what happened**

## 🌐 Network & Connectivity Issues

### WiFi Connection Problems

**Symptoms:** "No Connection" or "Server Error" messages
**Solutions:**
1. **Check WiFi symbol** on device - should show strong signal
2. **Turn WiFi off and on** in device settings
3. **Move closer to WiFi router**
4. **Try connecting to guest network** if available
5. **Restart device**
6. **Contact IT** - may be server issue

### Slow Response Times

**Symptoms:** Everything takes a long time to load
**Solutions:**
1. **Check WiFi signal strength**
2. **Close unnecessary apps**
3. **Wait during busy periods** - system may be overloaded
4. **Report persistent slowness** to supervisor
5. **Continue with critical tasks** and delay non-urgent work

## ⚡ Equipment Issues

### Handheld Device Battery Problems

**Symptoms:** Device dies quickly or won't charge
**Solutions:**
1. **Check charging cable** connections
2. **Try different charging dock/cable**
3. **Clean charging contacts** with dry cloth
4. **Replace battery** if removable
5. **Request spare device** from supervisor
6. **Keep devices plugged in** when not in use

### Scale/Weight Issues

**Symptoms:** Scale shows wrong weight or "Error"
**Solutions:**
1. **Check scale is level** and on stable surface
2. **Calibrate scale** using calibration weight
3. **Clean scale platform** of debris
4. **Check weight is within scale capacity**
5. **Zero/tare the scale** before weighing
6. **Contact maintenance** if readings still wrong

## 📞 When to Get Help

### Contact Supervisor When:
- Safety concerns or equipment malfunctions
- System errors affecting multiple operators
- Inventory discrepancies or missing items
- Process questions or unclear instructions
- Persistent equipment problems

### Contact IT Support When:
- Login issues or password problems
- Device hardware failures
- Network connectivity problems
- Software crashes or error messages
- Printer configuration issues

## 💡 Prevention Tips

### Daily Best Practices:
- **Charge devices** at end of shift
- **Clean scanners** with lens wipes
- **Report small issues** before they become big problems
- **Keep work area** clean and organized
- **Follow proper lifting** and safety procedures

### Equipment Care:
- **Handle devices gently** - they're expensive to replace
- **Keep food and drinks** away from equipment
- **Store devices** in designated areas when not in use
- **Report damage immediately** - don't try to fix it yourself

**Remember**: When in doubt, ask! It's better to get help than to make a mistake that affects productivity or safety. Your supervisor and IT team are there to support you in getting your job done efficiently and safely.
